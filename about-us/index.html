Who We Are

Built For The
Next Generation.

Touchline Sport Academy is a football development academy based in Dubai, created to give players a structured, positive and professional environment to develop their game.

From young beginners discovering football for the first time to experienced players looking to improve their performance, our programs are designed around the needs of each player.

We believe football is more than what happens during a training session. It teaches discipline, teamwork, confidence, resilience and responsibility — qualities that stay with players long after they leave the pitch.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Everything we do is built around creating better players, better teammates and better people.

Development

We focus on long-term player development rather than simply winning a single match. Every session has a purpose.

Teamwork

Football is a team sport. Players learn to communicate, support one another and understand their role within a team.

Character

Discipline, respect, resilience and confidence are an important part of every player's journey at Touchline.

Performance

Our training environment encourages players to challenge themselves and continuously improve their technical ability.

Passion

We want players to enjoy football. Passion and enjoyment create the foundation for consistent development.

Confidence

Players should feel comfortable making decisions, trying new things and expressing themselves on the pitch.
