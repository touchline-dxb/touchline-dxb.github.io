Frequently Asked Questions

What does a typical coaching day look like at TSA?

Sessions run in the late afternoons and evenings on weekdays, and during the day on weekends. You'll be running structured training sessions for mixed age groups (4–18+) across our Al Nahda and Al Warqa locations. Each session has a warm-up, skill drills, small-sided games, and a cool-down debrief.

Do part-time coaches need a UAE visa?

Yes — part-time coach roles (Football & Basketball, 2 days/week) require you to already hold a valid UAE residency visa. This is perfect for PE teachers, fitness instructors, or coaches with a primary job. Full-time roles may include visa consideration for qualified candidates.

What coaching qualifications do I need?

A coaching licence (UEFA Level 1/2, AFC, or national equivalent) is preferred but not always required — we value real experience with youth players. What matters most is your energy, personality, and genuine passion for developing kids through football.

How do I apply?

Use the application form above — choose Senior Coach or Assistant Coach, add your photo, fill in your details and upload your CV. We review all applications and aim to respond within 3–5 business days.

Are there non-coaching roles available?

We periodically hire for academy management, sales and admin roles. Our current drive is for Senior and Assistant Coaches — check back on this page for future openings.
